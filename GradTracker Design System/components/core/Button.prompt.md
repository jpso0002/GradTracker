The pill CTA — use for every action; `variant="primary"` appears at most once per band or panel.

```jsx
<Button iconLeft="sparkles">Scan inbox</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" size="sm">Snooze</Button>
<Button variant="quiet" iconLeft="filter">Filter</Button>
```

Radius is always `--radius-pill`; padding never goes below 8px 16px at `md`. `lg` (44px) is the mobile touch size. Press state darkens rather than shrinks.
